Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you visit

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch and we'll be glad to help.

How often should I visit a dentist?

It varies from person to person. Regular checkups are commonly recommended, and your dentist will suggest an interval that suits your teeth, gums and overall oral health.

What should I expect during my first visit?

We'll start by asking about your dental and medical history and anything that's concerning you. Your dentist will then examine your teeth and gums and talk you through what they find and any options worth considering. [Add clinic-specific details, e.g. what to bring.]

Do you offer teeth whitening?

Yes. Professional teeth whitening is part of our cosmetic dentistry services. A consultation comes first, so your dentist can check that whitening is suitable for you and explain what to expect.

How can I request an appointment?

Fill in the appointment request form with your preferred date and time, or call us on [Phone Number]. A request isn't a confirmed booking until we've contacted you.
